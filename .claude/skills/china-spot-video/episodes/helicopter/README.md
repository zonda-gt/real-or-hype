## Trip guide outline (DM page for "Comment HELICOPTER")
- **Name for Trip.com / Dianping / DiDi:** 新空直升机 (Shanghai New Sky Helicopter). Bases: 新空直升机(龙华飞行基地) 徐汇区龙腾大道2500号 (Metro 11 Longyao Rd, ~10 min walk; day only); 新空直升机复兴岛飞行基地 杨浦区复兴岛 (Metro 12 Fuxing Island + short taxi; day + night); 新空直升机(星野飞行基地) 浦东 near Disney (Disney/fireworks flights).
- **Prices (checked 20 Nov 2026, from 2024-2026 listings):** Huangpu River skyline flight about ¥1,680-1,980 pp by day (about $240-280), about ¥2,180 at night (2025); charters for up to 6 from about ¥10,800 (10 min). Short "experience" hops ¥298-580 don't reach the Bund.
- **Booking:** search 新空直升机 on Dianping/Meituan/Douyin or Ctrip; book 1-3 days ahead (3+ for night/holidays); give your ID at booking.
- **Foreign visitors:** the rules conflict (one operator notice says foreign nationals can't fly). Message the operator first (Ctrip lists +86 400 920 8969 / +86 173 0182 1529) and get written confirmation that your passport is accepted; private charters via international agencies are an alternative.
- **Weather:** rain, low cloud or ATC can cancel; you get a new slot or a full refund. Clearest months Oct-Dec and Mar-May.
- **On the day:** arrive early for ID check and security (no power banks, lighters, liquids); tie long hair, no hats; sit by the window (both sides see the towers on the out-and-back route).
- **Nearby:** West Bund museums (Longhua), Yangpu riverside / Fuxing Island, then the Bund at night.
