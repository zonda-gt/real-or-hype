## Trip guide (DM page outline)
**水裹汤泉(五角场店)**, 上海市杨浦区淞沪路111号 3–4楼 (五角场万达广场). Show this to a taxi driver, or search 水裹汤泉 五角场 in DiDi or Dianping.
- **Metro:** Line 10 to Jiangwan Stadium (江湾体育场). Follow signs to Wanda Plaza; it is a few minutes' walk. Driving: 3 h free parking.
- **Hours:** 24 h. Daytime tickets 6–16 h (weekday 16 h, weekend/holiday 6 h). Overnight: arrive after 9 pm, leave by 11 am–1 pm.
- **Prices (checked 20 Nov 2026):** about ¥273–299 a person (about $40). Meituan/Dianping deals are cheapest. Overnight +¥69 with breakfast; Fri/Sat overnight ¥344. Kids 80–140 cm pay the child price.
- **Included:** pools, saunas and salt cave, unlimited fruit, drinks and ice cream, pyjamas, towels, toiletries and skincare, rest areas, tents, cinema.
- **Extra:** meals (about ¥30–60), massage and scrubs, arcade, KTV, billiards. Everything goes on your wristband; pay when you leave.
- **For foreigners:** bring your passport (staff register it at the desk). Bathing is nude and men and women are separate. If you're shy, wrap a towel on the way in. Pay with Alipay or WeChat Pay.
- **Best time:** weekdays, arrive by 10–11 am for a cubby or tent; weekends are packed.
- **Overnight tip:** earplugs and an eye mask; avoid the beds next to the restaurant.
- **Alternative:** the Hongqiao branch (龙湖虹桥天街B馆B1) is 24 h and a short walk from Hongqiao Railway Station, so it works for a long layover. Leave your luggage at the front desk.
- **Nearby:** Wujiaochang shopping, Fudan University campus walk.
- Apps: Alipay, DiDi, Dianping/Meituan for the deal. Footer: "Want a full China itinerary? Reply PLAN".
