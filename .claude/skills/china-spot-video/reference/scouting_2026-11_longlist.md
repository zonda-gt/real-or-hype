# China "internet-famous" longlist, Nov 2026 (3 methods × 50)

Places that are viral on Douyin / Xiaohongshu but little known abroad. Scouted 17 Nov 2026, nationwide; none of the 60 places already in `places.csv` is repeated. **Unverified longlist:** check opening status, prices and facts before scripting an episode.

- **A. Douyin (TikHub):** 80 keyword searches (most-liked, last 180 days), ~560 videos, grouped by the tagged Douyin location. Evidence = likes on the top video + the location's "want to go" (人想去) count.
- **B. Doubao (Seed 2.1 Pro + web search):** 12 themed queries, 6 places each. Doubao's own claims and sources; not checked.
- **C. Web research (Claude):** Chinese + English web search, each place cross-checked against at least one source. Skews more mainstream-in-China than A and B (Zibo BBQ, Cun Chao, etc.).

Found by two methods: Jilong Castle (A+B), Wuyuan sinkhole library (A+B), Meixin Wine Town Fuling (A+B), Changbaishan green-train hotel (A+B), Pingtan blue tears (B+C), Wangxian Valley (B+C), Wansui Mountain Wuxia City (B+C), Xunpu hairpin-flower village (B+C). C #1 is the Hangzhou disaster-simulation base from the creator's own example.
