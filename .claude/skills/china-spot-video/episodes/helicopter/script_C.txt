Four things to know before you book a helicopter over Shanghai. One: the skyline flight follows the Huangpu River, past the Bund and Lujiazui, and it lasts about ten minutes. A seat costs about two hundred eighty dollars. Two: night flights cost a little more, but every tower is lit up. Three: book one to three days ahead. Rain or low cloud can ground the helicopter, and then you get a new slot or a refund. Four: if you're not Chinese, ask about passport rules before you pay, because some tickets only take Chinese ID.
