## Doubts and flags
- **Sleep No More (McKinnon Hotel) has closed.** Its final show was on 30 Aug 2026 (Sixth Tone, 10 Sep 2026). Row 14 replaces it.
- **I could not find a paid robot-boxing venue open to the public.** WAIC rings, the IFC mall pop-up (Jun 2025) and URKL Baoshan (Sep 2026) were demos or events for spectators. Qianxing's "Iron Armor Battle Arena" (TechNode, Aug 2026) has no confirmed Shanghai location.
- **Main table has 30 paid rows** (1–15, 18–20, 23–34). Rows 16, 17, 21 and 22 are free or ordinary retail, so they are in Extras with E5 (monthly robot rental, not practical for a visitor).
- **Seasonal or possibly ended:** SIC "Pole to Win" (row 19) was announced only through Nov 2025, so 2026 dates are not confirmed. Tiger Craft (row 23) ended its 2026 season on 27 Oct. Taixu (row 14) runs only to 29 Nov 2026.
- **Old or weak sources:**
  - Wei Wei Caier (row 13) was last verified in 2019.
  - The massage venue prices (rows 11–12) date from 2020; the 2026 ranges are estimates from a guide.
  - Mahjong, kung fu and Shanghainese-cooking prices come from undated reseller listings.
  - The Cici's listing window closed on 31 Jul 2026, though the studio still lists on Cookly/Klook.
- **Foreigner friction:**
  - Robotaxi and test-drive apps are in Chinese.
  - Porsche requires a "valid driving licence"; I could not confirm whether a foreign licence is accepted.
  - Sources disagree on whether a foreign licence or a Chinese temporary permit is needed to rent an EV such as an SU7.
- **Pony.ai** ran only on weekdays, 07:30–21:30, at the 2025 launch. Apollo Go's Shanghai service was originally in Jiading. A report of an Oct 2026 expansion (af.net) also names Waymo and is unverified.
- **New rows 25–34:**
  - The helicopter price (row 29) is from the Sep 2024 launch, and the 2026 reseller window closed in May 2026. A Global Harbor (Putuo) route charged ¥650–2,520 (May 2025), but its route approval ran only to 31 May 2025.
  - The murder-mystery club's latest event found is from Jan 2025.
  - Pottery, bike-tour and opera-workshop prices come from undated reseller listings.
  - The crab feast price is per dish, not a set menu, and hairy-crab season ends around late Nov.
  - ERA (row 31) is a show ticket rather than a hands-on activity.
- **Searched but not added:**
  - Paid eVTOL flights: EHang trials at Longhua, but no public fares found.
  - Dianshan Lake sailing: clubs exist, but no prices found.
  - Sidecar tours: only 2019 prices found.
  - Longhua acupuncture: no reliable price found.
