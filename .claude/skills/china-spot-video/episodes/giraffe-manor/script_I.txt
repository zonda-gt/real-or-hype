Two baby giraffes have been born at a hotel in China. The first, in April 2024, was six foot one on day one. The second arrived this June. Here it is, just born, right beside its mom. Keepers bottle-feed the babies, and every morning starts with a head rub. And the grown-ups? They come to breakfast. One pushes its whole head through the window, and takes a carrot right from your hand. Outside, they walk right past you on the bamboo trail.
