# XMEDIC health check-up (幸元会 XMEDIC 健康体检), Shanghai

The creator's own visit, filmed 13 Nov 2026 (39 clips in OneDrive folder `素材`; 1080×1920, 29.97 fps, SDR BT.709).
Style reference: @wynnejael "$400 health check" reel, https://www.instagram.com/reel/Da-anSGp_y4/ (85.9 s, 345 words, ~248 wpm, 49 shots, median 1.43 s).
Unlike the usual china-spot episodes, **the creator was really there**, so the narration is first person ("I") in his voice.

Two variants (the creator asked for two, not three):
- **A, "The VIP walkthrough"**: her structure. A surprising first-person hook, the backstory, the visit step by step, a pivot ("But here's the part…"), an honest line, the wrap-up. It answers "what is it like?"
- **B, "What I expected vs what I got"**: a contrast story ("Instead of X, Y"). Each line is a cliché about hospitals against a shot from the visit. It answers "is it better than a hospital?"
