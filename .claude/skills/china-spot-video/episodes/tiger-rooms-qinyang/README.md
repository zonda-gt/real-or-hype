# Tiger-view rooms, Qinyang Hesheng Forest Zoo, Henan (china-spot-video, Nov 2026)
The brief called it "Qinyang Swan Lake Zoo (沁阳天鹅湖动物园)". The zoo's current name is **沁阳和生森林动物园** (also written 沁阳和生森林动物乐园; operator 沁阳市和生森林动物乐园有限公司, founded 2021). It used to sit at **天鹅湖 / 沁阳市天鹅湖生态园** (Swan Lake) and has since moved to **西万镇** (a guest comment and the zoo's own reply under Douyin 7682751888701345039; its older Douyin account is still called 沁阳市天鹅湖生态园). The rooms are called **虎景房** (tiger view) and **狮景房** (lion view). Ctrip still lists a "天鹅湖动物园" entry for Qinyang.

Verdict on the claim: **real, and still running in Nov 2026.** Two corrections: tigers mostly **rest at night** (staff, several outlets), so not "pacing all night"; and **¥168 was the April 2026 weekday price**. Holidays and packages have been ¥298 (with meals). Check the price by phone before quoting it in the guide.

## Three angles (chosen before scripting)
- **A, "Sleep with tigers" (experience walkthrough):** the draw is how a night feels: the bed, the shower, the tiger lying at the glass, the morning walk-up. The footage covers each beat.
  - Hook: 2×2 collage of four wow shots (TL tiger lying at the glass beside a guest's hand, TR tiger's face pressed to the glass at dusk, BL legs on the bed with a tiger walking past, BR white tiger face on). Title "CHINA'S / Tiger Hotel". Medium pace (about 1.5 s a shot), handwritten labels.
- **B, "Australia vs China" (price comparison):** Jamala Wildlife Lodge in Canberra sells glass-walled rooms onto tigers from about A$1,795 a night. Here it starts at about $24 with zoo tickets. Concrete, checked, and a big gap.
  - Hook: HERO, the single most wow shot: a tiger lying against the glass, face to face with the camera (LI 80.5), title "AUSTRALIA: $1,000+ / China: $24" moved to the top so the face stays clear. Fast list cuts (about 1.2 s), price cards.
- **C, "4 things before you book" (insider list):** practical: it is real, you book by phone in Chinese, tigers sleep at night so wake early, and it is far from Shanghai (train route).
  - Hook: HERO 4K shot of a tiger walking straight up to the window (T4K 4.3), title "4 THINGS / before you book" at the top. Relaxed pace (about 1.9 s), numbered #1–#4 stickers, route card.
- **Keyword:** TIGER (not in ChinaPal `code-rules.ts` as of 18 Nov 2026; giraffe, castle, dragon, silo, wow are taken).

沁阳和生森林动物园 虎景房, Qinyang, Jiaozuo, Henan
