For about twenty five dollars, you can dance in Shanghai's most famous 1930s ballroom. It's the Paramount, near Jing'an Temple, and it opened in 1933. Walk in under the chandelier, and climb the red staircase. At two o'clock, the ballroom opens. Gold ceilings. Crystal chandeliers. A live jazz band on stage. Find a table, and order tea and snacks. Then the floor fills up. Waltz. Rumba. Salsa. Ball gowns and sharp suits. The floor is sprung, so it gives a little with every step. The dancing runs until five.
