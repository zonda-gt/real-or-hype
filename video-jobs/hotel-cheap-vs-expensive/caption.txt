¥200 vs ¥6,000 hotel in Shanghai 🇨🇳 Is the expensive one worth 30x more?

🏨 VX Gefei Hotel, North Bund (Huangxing Rd): ¥200+/night (≈ $30) on a group-buy deal (¥400+ for 2 nights). Clean, modern, robot butler. No view.
🏨 Sheraton Pudong Riverside, Pudong: ¥500+/night (≈ $75) on a deal (¥1,000+ for 2 nights, breakfast for 2). Bund across the river from the window. Classic, older decor.
🏨 Banyan Tree Shanghai On The Bund, Hongkou: ¥6,000+/night (≈ $900), Supreme River View Suite. A full suite and a bathtub facing the skyline.

My pick for value: the Sheraton 👀

Prices are what the Douyin creators quoted. The two cheaper ones are promo package prices and change by date. Footage: Douyin creators, credited in the comments.
