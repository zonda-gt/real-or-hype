## Trip guide (for the "Comment PARAMOUNT" DM page)
百乐门 Paramount, 上海市静安区愚园路218号 (show this to the taxi driver)
- **Metro:** Jing'an Temple (Lines 2, 7, 14), about 3 min walk.
- **Afternoon tea dance:** 14:00–17:00, live jazz band, about ¥180 / about $25 per person (checked Nov 2026: AFP Dec 2025, tour listing Jun 2026). Reported daily; call to confirm the day.
- **Book ahead:** +86 21 6249 8866 (or the front desk). A visitor in Sep 2026 was turned away without a booking.
- **Dress:** smart; no shorts, no slippers. Regulars wear qipao, gowns and suits.
- **Dancing:** bring a partner, or book a ballroom teacher to lead you; otherwise sit with tea and watch.
- **Kids:** visitors say under-18s can't join the tea dance; there is a separate visit ticket.
- **Evening:** dinner + shows (pricier; check).
- **Nearby:** Jing'an Temple, Yuyuan Road (愚园路) walk, Jing'an Park; in-house 1930s costume photo studio (百乐门民国妆造).
