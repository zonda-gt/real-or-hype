## Three angles (chosen before scripting)
- **A, "Idol for a day" (the transformation walkthrough):** the strongest draw is watching an ordinary visitor become an idol, step by step: lookbook → makeup → hair → wardrobe → pink wall → fan-sign → backstage → big screen.
  - Hook: a 2×2 collage of finished results with a serif title. Medium pace (about 1.2 s a shot), handwritten labels.
- **B, "What $50 gets you" (price breakdown):** the studio's own price list is concrete, and the deal is the surprise: ¥349 for makeup, hair, an outfit and 3 hours on 12 sets.
  - Hook: a full-screen hero (a guest's face on the giant LED screen) with "$50" in the title. A fast list (about 0.8 s a shot) and three price cards ($50 / $100 / $18).
- **C, "Even Koreans come here" (K-pop fan angle):** Korean visitors fly in and try it, and every set copies a real K-pop TV moment (Music Core pink wall, Inkigayo MC desk, fan-sign, stage). It answers "is it the real deal?" for fans.
  - Hook: the Korean visitor waving at the Inkigayo set. Relaxed pace (about 1.5 s), numbered #1–#4 stickers.
- **Keyword:** IDOL (the same on all three).

## Trip guide outline (for the "Comment IDOL" DM page)
- **Chengo爱豆体验馆**, 上海市浦东新区临沂北路150号2号楼204室. Show this to a DiDi driver; it is about 4 km south of Lujiazui in Pudong. Nearest metro: check Amap (Tangqiao / Lancun Rd area, Lines 4/6; unverified).
- **Prices** (studio list, checked 20 Nov 2026; Korean style):
  - ¥129: entry, 12 sets for 3 h
  - ¥179: + 1 outfit
  - **¥349: + makeup + hair (about $50)**
  - ¥449: + 1 h of phone photos by staff
  - ¥699: + 1 h DSLR photographer, 6 edited photos
  - Extras: outfit ¥50, wig ¥30, photocards ¥10 each
- **Booking:** makeup and photographers on the studio's WeChat mini-program, the day before; arrive at the start of your slot (30 min late and you can lose it). Entry only: walk in.
- **Time:** about 1.5 h for makeup and hair, then 3 h on the sets. Big screen free 11:00–20:00.
- **Hours:** 9:00–21:00 (weekends to 21:50).
- **Tips:**
  - bring your own coloured contacts and earrings
  - weekday mornings are calmer; weekends sell out
  - English isn't guaranteed: have a translation app ready
  - print your photos as photocards on the way out
- **Apps:** Alipay or WeChat Pay, DiDi, WeChat (for booking).
- **Nearby:** Lujiazui skyline (10 min by car); Century Park.
