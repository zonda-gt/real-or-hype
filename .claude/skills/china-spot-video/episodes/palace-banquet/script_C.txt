Four things to know before you go to Shanghai's palace banquet dinner show. It's called Yan Tianxia, on the West Bund. One. Book your seat ahead, and think about the front row. Only the front row gets the hand washing ritual. Two. Arrive an hour early to dress up. A silk robe plus hair and makeup is about thirty dollars. Three. It's not only the Tang dynasty. Seven acts take you from ancient China all the way to a nineteen thirties Shanghai nightclub. Four. Come for the show, not the food. The dishes look great, but guests say the portions are small. Dinner runs about seventy to a hundred dollars a person. Comment the word banquet, and we'll send you the full guide.
