## Script
- Hook from frame 1, first person, a surprising claim with a concrete detail: "I didn't expect to find a giant LEGO flower wave in Shanghai." / "Can you believe, in Shanghai, you get your CT results explained to you within five minutes of the scan?" / "There's a seafood restaurant hidden in a Shanghai basement."
- Next breath: a number or social proof ("over 380,000 LEGO pieces", "people start lining up at 5 p.m.", "my German friend swore…").
- Name + where + until when by ~10 s.
- Walkthrough in the order you experience it: "From far away… Get closer… Keep walking… Then… After that…"
- One pivot: "But here's the part that actually blew my mind."
- One honest reaction line, no more ("This part was actually pretty cool.").
- A next step that turns it into an outing ("walk the West Bund riverside, an easy half-day").
- CTA tied to the series promise: "Follow me for more Shanghai spots worth exploring."
- Plain but dense: ~10–12 words per sentence, sensory adjectives for food, no jokes or bait formulas.
