# 30 paid local experiences in Shanghai (scouted 17 Nov 2026)

Services you pay for and do, not sightseeing tickets. Four methods:
- **D**: Doubao (Seed 2.1 Pro + web search, 5 themed queries)
- **W**: web research (WebSearch + page fetches)
- **DY**: Douyin search via TikHub (30 keywords)
- **X**: Xiaohongshu search via TikHub `app_v2/search_notes` (same 30 keywords)

Prices are as the source showed them on the date given. They are not re-checked today, so confirm before filming or booking. Raw outputs are in `work/shexp/` (gitignored).
