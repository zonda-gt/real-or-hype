# Yan Tianxia · Yanle (宴天下·宴乐), Shanghai's palace-banquet dinner show (china-spot-video, Nov 2026)
Brief: "imperial palace-banquet dinner show". Two Shanghai candidates were checked:
- **宴天下·宴乐(徐汇店)**, chosen. It has about 25 Douyin videos tagged at the Xuhui venue, five of them over 10k likes, plus many XHS guides with prices and seating, and English listings on Trip.com and Klook.
- 琅宫宴(外滩店), Nanjing East Rd: opened in Jul 2026, has 9–19 videos tagged at the venue (top 11k) and sells cheaper deals (about ¥149–298). It has fewer facts and less footage.

The chain 宴天下 started in Luoyang and also runs Xi'an, Nanjing, Tangshan, Chengdu and Shenzhen venues. **All footage below is tagged 宴天下·宴乐(徐汇店).** No Luoyang or Shenzhen clips are used.

- **A, "Dress up and dine like an emperor" (walkthrough):** the experience in order (robe and makeup, the round hall, hand washing, the acts, the dishes, the 1930s finale). It is the most visual story, and the footage covers every step.
  - Hook: 2×2 collage + "SHANGHAI'S / Palace Banquet". Medium pace (about 1.3 s a shot), handwritten labels.
- **B, "A $70 dinner that's actually a show" (price / value):** the price punchline. Dinner starts at about ¥498 (about $70) and includes a 90-minute show, with honest small portions.
  - Hook: a full-screen umbrella acrobat + title. Fast list cuts (about 1.0 s), price cards.
- **C, "4 things before you go" (insider list):** practical tips plus the honest heads-up: book the front row for hand washing, arrive early for costumes (¥200), not only Tang (it ends in 1930s Shanghai), come for the show, not the food.
  - Hook: a full-screen 360° hall + title. Relaxed pace (about 1.6 s), numbered #1–#4 labels.

## Trip guide outline (DM for BANQUET)
- **Taxi / Trip.com name:** 宴天下·宴乐(徐汇店), 徐汇区龙启路158号灿星大厦1幢2层 (West Bund Dream Center).
- **Metro:** Line 11 Yunjin Road, Exit 4, about 2 min walk.
- **Seatings:** lunch 12:00, weekend afternoon 15:30, dinner 19:00, each about 1 h 50 min. Arrive 30 min early, or 1–2 h early for costumes.
- **Prices (checked 20 Nov 2026, list, per person):** dinner ¥498–688 (about $70–$97), lunch ¥398–588, by seat ring. Front row is the most expensive and the only row with the hand-washing ritual. Deals sometimes appear on Douyin and Meituan.
- **Book:** WeChat mini program 宴天下宴乐 (¥100 deposit, refunded at check-in, up to 15 days ahead), or Trip.com / Klook in English.
- **Costume:** robe ¥100 + hair & makeup ¥100.
- **Tips:**
  - Row 2 is best for photos and row 4 for value. Avoid the seats by the performer entrances.
  - Eat a snack first, because the portions are small.
  - Answer the trivia questions for a small prize.
  - Stay for the cast photo on stage.
- **Nearby:** West Bund riverside walk, Long Museum West Bund, Yuz Museum, West Bund Museum (Centre Pompidou).
- **Apps:** Alipay, DiDi, Trip.com.
