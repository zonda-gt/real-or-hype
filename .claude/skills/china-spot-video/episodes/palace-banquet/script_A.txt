In Shanghai, there's a dinner where you dress up like Tang dynasty royalty, and the show happens all around your table. It's called Yan Tianxia, on the West Bund. First, pick a silk robe, and get your hair and makeup done. Then you walk into a round hall, wrapped in a three sixty degree screen. Sit in the front row, and someone washes your hands for you. Then come seven acts in ninety minutes. Dancers fly over your head on wires. Snow falls on the stage. And every act brings a new dish, like Buddha Jumps Over the Wall in golden broth. It all ends in nineteen thirties Shanghai, under a disco ball. Dinner starts at about seventy dollars. Come for the show, not to get full.
