Four things to know before you visit Shanghai's Hogwarts bakery. One: it's not official Harry Potter. It's an Italian bakery called Apoli, dressed up like a castle. Two: you can't book a table. It gets packed, so come on a weekday morning. Three: it's self service. Grab a tray and tongs, fill it up, then queue to pay. Four: it's right across from Wukang Mansion, one of the most photographed buildings in Shanghai, so do both. Most pastries cost less than three dollars.
