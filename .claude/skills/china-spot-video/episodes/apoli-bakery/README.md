# APOLI ITABAKERY, Shanghai's "Hogwarts bakery" (china-spot-video, Nov 2026)
The creator asked for "Apollo Cafe, the Harry Potter Hogwarts cafe in Shanghai". There is no Apollo café. This is **APOLI ITABAKERY** (AP意大利料理面包坊): Douyin and Xiaohongshu call it 霍格沃茨面包坊 or 哈利波特魔法面包屋. It is an Italian bakery with a castle interior, **not** licensed or official Harry Potter. The videos say "the bakery people call Shanghai's Hogwarts", never "Harry Potter café".

## Trip guide (the DM links the ChinaPal page)
APOLI ITABAKERY, 长宁区兴国路380号 (武康大楼斜对面)
- **Metro:** Jiaotong University, Exit 7, about 6 min.
- **Hours:** about 9:00–21:00.
- **Booking:** walk in; weekday mornings are calmest.
- **How it works:** tray + tongs, then pay at the till.
- **Payment:** Alipay / WeChat.
- **Order:** pistachio tart, Roman matcha cream bun, chicken & truffle bread (warm the savoury ones).
- Then photos at Wukang Mansion.
