# Where to do them: shop names and addresses (20 Nov 2026)

These are the place-specific experiences from the four lists (D = Doubao, W = web, DY = Douyin, X = Xiaohongshu).

Addresses come from three places:
- Douyin location tags on videos about the shop: `scripts/dypoi.py "<name>"`
- Xiaohongshu notes
- The Doubao and web lists

They are not checked on a map. Confirm opening and booking on Dianping before going.

Left out, because many shops do the same thing: massage, foot massage, head spa, ear cleaning, perler beads, bead bracelets, generic pottery, cheap trial classes (tennis, boxing, drums), self-service game rooms, murder-mystery games, and tours with no fixed place. One exception: bathhouses are listed with two examples.
