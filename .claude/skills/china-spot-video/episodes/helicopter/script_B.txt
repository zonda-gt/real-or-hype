A helicopter tour over Manhattan costs about three hundred dollars with fees. Over Shanghai, a seat costs about the same, for around ten minutes. So which skyline wins? In Shanghai, the flight runs straight down the Huangpu River, about three hundred meters up. On one bank, the Bund, built about a hundred years ago. On the other, Lujiazui. Shanghai Tower, the tallest building in China. The bottle opener. Jin Mao. And the Oriental Pearl, right under your feet. Then, after dark, the whole skyline lights up.
