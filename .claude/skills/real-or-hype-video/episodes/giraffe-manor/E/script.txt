There's a hotel in China where giraffes join you for breakfast. It's called Giraffe Manor, in Huzhou, about two hours from Shanghai. From the restaurant, you watch them walk across the field. Then one stops at your window and pushes its whole head inside. Hold out a carrot, and it takes it right from your hand. Outside, capybaras soak in the pond, raccoons climb the branches, and cranes walk through the mist. You sleep in a villa in the middle of a bamboo forest. But here's the part nobody expects.
