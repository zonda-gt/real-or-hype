Four things to know before you book China's tiger view rooms. One: it's real. A zoo in Henan has about thirty rooms with glass walls, and tigers on the other side. Two: you book by phone, in Chinese. Rooms start at about twenty-four dollars with two zoo tickets, and holidays cost more. Three: the tigers mostly sleep at night, so set an alarm for the morning. Four: it's far from Shanghai.
