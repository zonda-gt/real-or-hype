Forget the hotel. In Shanghai, you can spend the night in a bathhouse. Arrive in the evening, swap your shoes for a wristband, and change into pajamas. Soak in the hot pools, then lie in a salt cave until you're sleepy. Midnight snack? Durian, cherries, cold drinks and ice cream, all included. When you're ready, pick a bed. A tent, or a recliner in the big rest hall. In the morning, breakfast is included too. A weekday ticket plus the overnight fee comes to about fifty dollars. Light sleeper? Bring earplugs, because you share the room with strangers. This is Shuiguo, in Shanghai. Comment the word bathhouse, and we'll send you the full guide.
